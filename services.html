Your First Visit

What to expect

Whether it's your first time in church or your first time with us, we want you to feel at home from the moment you arrive.

  • A warm welcome from our host and hospitality teams
  • Uplifting contemporary praise and heartfelt worship
  • Practical, Bible-based teaching you can live out
  • A friendly family ready to journey with you
