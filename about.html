Who We Are

Rooted in faith, resting in grace.

We are a vibrant Church of God Mission International family in Port Harcourt, raising mature disciples of Christ and making room for every weary heart.

"How good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity." — Psalm 133:1

Our Story

A church raised in the CGMi heritage

The Rest Place is a branch of the Church of God Mission International (CGMi) — a movement birthed by the late Archbishop Benson Idahosa to carry the gospel of faith, healing and the Spirit across Nigeria and the world.

Known and loved as a lively, youth-driven assembly in the heart of Port Harcourt, we are "perfect for the young and young at heart." Our doors and our arms are open to everyone.

  • A living heritage of revival, prayer and missions
  • An atmosphere of joy, worship and the Word
  • A family where you are welcomed as you are

Why We Exist

Our mission & values

Our Mission

To create a welcoming place where weary souls encounter the love of God, young believers are discipled, and every person discovers their purpose in Christ.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of mature disciples who carry the presence of God into their families, workplaces and communities across the world.

Our Heart

Rest. We believe coming to Jesus is the truest rest — so we build atmospheres of peace, praise and the presence of God.
