Welcome

A vibrant church for the young and the young at heart

The Rest Place is a lively family of believers committed to raising mature disciples of Christ. Our atmosphere is filled with life-changing worship, the undiluted Word of God and genuine friendship.

  • Life-changing worship and the living Word, three times weekly
  • A joyful, youth-driven community ready to welcome you
  • Ministries for every season of your walk with God
